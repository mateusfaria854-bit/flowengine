## Future commercial alternative licensing

A managed Cloud service can be sold while complying with AGPL. A separate commercial license may be offered in the future only for material whose rights holders have authorized such licensing. This is a plan, not a currently available license or offer.

Before merging substantive external code or documentation, adopt a legally reviewed contributor agreement that grants the necessary rights for alternative licensing; maintain evidence of consent and provenance. Until then, defer such merges. Review dependencies, transitive licenses and compatibility; keep third-party notices and inventory. Do not assume that separate deployment, APIs or a package boundary automatically exempt a combined work.

This policy is a project design decision and does not replace qualified legal review before commercialization.
